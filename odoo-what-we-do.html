Our Process

How Our Development Process Works

From initial requirements to ongoing support — a structured, transparent process that keeps your project on track and on budget.

01

Requirement Analysis

We start by thoroughly understanding your business requirements through detailed consultations, identifying the specific functionalities and features you need from Odoo.

02

Design & Planning

Based on the analysis, we develop a comprehensive development plan with detailed specifications, timelines, and milestones to ensure a smooth, predictable process.

03

Custom Development

Our experienced developers build new features and modules — or enhance existing ones — following best practices to deliver high-quality, maintainable code.

04

Testing & Quality Assurance

We conduct thorough testing across functionality, integration, and performance to guarantee that all custom developments work seamlessly within your Odoo environment.

05

Deployment

Once testing is complete, we deploy to your live environment with a carefully managed transition — minimizing disruption and ensuring business continuity throughout.

06

Support & Maintenance

Post-deployment, we provide ongoing support and maintenance — addressing issues promptly, delivering regular updates, and ensuring your system continues to perform optimally.
