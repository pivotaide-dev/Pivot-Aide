Methodology

Our Migration Process — Step by Step

We follow a proven, low-risk migration methodology that keeps your business running during transition.

01

Discovery & Data Audit

We take stock of everything in your current system — chart of accounts, transaction history, open balances, contacts, inventory, and custom fields.

02

Data Mapping & Transformation

We map your existing data structure to Odoo's schema, resolving naming differences, currency formats, tax codes, and account classifications.

03

Sandbox Migration & Testing

All data is migrated into a test environment first. We validate record counts, trial balance totals, open AR/AP, and report outputs.

04

Integration Configuration

We configure all third-party connections — payment gateways, eCommerce platforms, payroll providers, bank feeds — and run end-to-end tests.

05

Cutover & Go-Live

On go-live day, we execute a final delta sync to capture last-minute transactions, flip the switch to live Odoo, and stand by on-site/remote.

06

Post-Migration Reconciliation & Support

We reconcile opening balances, verify integration sync logs, and provide a 30-day hypercare window — dedicated support to resolve any issues.
