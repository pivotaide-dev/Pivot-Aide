Our Approach

How We Train Your Team

A structured, step-by-step approach that ensures your team not only knows how to use Odoo — but knows how to use it well.

01

Needs Assessment

We assess your training requirements — understanding your business processes, user roles, and current proficiency levels with Odoo.

02

Customized Training Plan

We develop a tailored plan covering modules, delivery schedules, and methods — aligned to your team's schedule and learning style.

03

Hands-On Training Sessions

Our instructors conduct practical, real-world training sessions — on-site or remote — helping users apply Odoo's core functions.

04

Role-Based Training

We deliver specialized training for each user type within your organization — administrators, managers, end-users, and IT staff.

05

Interactive Workshops

Beyond formal sessions, we run Q&A workshops to address specific user queries and challenges — reinforcing real system confidence.

06

Comprehensive Documentation

We provide detailed user manuals, quick reference guides, and process documentation that teams can return to anytime.

07

Customized Documentation

We create documentation reflecting your unique business workflows — so your team has precise, role-relevant instructions.

08

Ongoing Training & Updates

As your business evolves and new Odoo features launch, we provide updated training sessions and refreshed documentation.

09

Performance Assessments

We conduct periodic assessments to evaluate training effectiveness — identifying skill gaps and measuring proficiency growth over time.
