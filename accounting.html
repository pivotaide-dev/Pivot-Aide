Reporting Services

What We Deliver

Comprehensive, audit-ready deliverables tailored for executive oversight, board reporting, and operational growth.

01

Financial Statement Preparation

  • Balance Sheet: A clear snapshot of assets, liabilities, and equity — prepared accurately and on schedule.
  • Income Statement: Revenue, expenses, and net profit presented clearly for management and tax purposes.
  • Cash Flow Statement: Understand where cash is coming from and where it's going, every period.
02

Management Reporting

  • KPI Dashboards: Custom views of the metrics your leadership team uses to run the business.
  • Department & Project Reports: Break performance down by team, location, or service line.
  • Executive Summaries: Concise monthly narratives that translate numbers into plain-language insights.
03

Variance Analysis

  • Actuals vs. Budget: Side-by-side comparison of what was planned against what actually happened.
  • Trend Identification: Spot recurring gaps early so you can course-correct before they compound.
  • Root Cause Review: We help you understand why variances occurred, not just that they did.
04

Budgeting & Forecasting

  • Operating Budget Build: Annual budgets by department, project, or cost center inside Odoo.
  • Rolling Forecasts: Dynamic projections updated as conditions change — not a static spreadsheet.
  • Scenario Analysis: Model best-case, base-case, and downside scenarios to stress-test your plans.
05

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

  • KPI Definition: Identify the financial metrics that actually matter for your business model.
  • Odoo Dashboard Setup: Configure live KPI tracking directly inside your Odoo environment.
  • Monthly KPI Review: Regular check-ins to assess performance and adjust targets as needed.
06

Cash Flow Analysis

  • 13-Week Cash Flow: Near-term visibility into inflows and outflows so you're never caught off guard.
  • Cash Conversion Cycle: Understand how fast your business converts sales into cash on hand.
  • Liquidity Planning: Identify potential shortfalls early and plan around them proactively.
07

Cost & Profitability Analysis

  • Cost Structure Review: Identify where money is going and where efficiency can be improved.
  • Gross Margin by Product/Service: Know which offerings are actually profitable — and which aren't.
  • Pricing Support: Use margin analysis to inform confident pricing decisions backed by real data.
08

Ad-Hoc & Consolidated Reporting

  • On-Demand Reports: Need a specific report for a board meeting, investor, or lender? We deliver it fast.
  • Multi-Entity Consolidation: For businesses with multiple locations or subsidiaries, we roll everything into one clean view.
  • Audit-Ready Packages: Pull together everything an auditor or CPA needs — organized and reconciled.
