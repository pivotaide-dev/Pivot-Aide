What We Implement

Full-Scope Odoo Accounting Setup

Complete end-to-end module configuration designed for accuracy, speed, and regulatory compliance.

01

Initial Setup & Configuration

  • Chart of Accounts: Built or restructured to match your business structure and reporting needs.
  • System Configuration: Fiscal year, currencies, journals, and accounting methods set up correctly from the start.
  • Tax Configuration: Sales tax, use tax, and applicable tax rules configured and state compliant.
02

Invoicing & Payments

  • Invoice Automation: Templated, branded invoices that go out on time without manual effort.
  • Payment Integration: Connect your preferred payment processor for streamlined receivables.
  • Recurring Billing: Automate invoices for retainer clients, subscriptions, or periodic service agreements.
03

Bank Reconciliation

  • Bank Feed Integration: Connect your bank accounts for automatic daily statement imports.
  • Reconciliation Automation: Rules-based matching that handles routine transactions automatically.
  • Exception Handling: Clear workflows for flagging and resolving items needing manual review.
04

Accounts Payable & Receivable

  • AP/AR Management: Structured workflows to ensure bills get paid and invoices get collected — on time.
  • Aging Reports: Clear visibility into what's owed and what you owe, always current.
  • Collections Workflow: Automated follow-up for overdue receivables to reduce write-offs.
05

Payroll Integration

  • Payroll Journal Entries: Seamless posting of payroll transactions from your payroll provider into Odoo.
  • Contractor & 1099 Tracking: Manage independent contractor payments and year-end 1099 data with accuracy.
  • Benefits & Deductions: Properly record employer contributions, garnishments, and deductions.
06

Tax Compliance & Year-End Close

  • Tax-Ready Books: Clean, reconciled records throughout the year — no last-minute scrambles.
  • Audit Trail: Every transaction logged and traceable, compliant with standard accounting requirements.
  • Year-End Close Process: We guide or execute the full close so your books are clean and CPA-ready.
