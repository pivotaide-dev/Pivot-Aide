Our Migration Process

Step-by-Step — Nothing Gets Lost

We follow a structured migration process that protects your data, minimizes disruption, and gets your team up and running in Odoo with confidence.

01

Assessment & Planning

  • Analyze your existing QuickBooks data and structure
  • Define clear objectives and migration scope
  • Build a detailed timeline and migration roadmap
02

Data Preparation

  • Export customers, vendors, products, invoices, and payments
  • Export chart of accounts, open balances, and transaction history
  • Clean and validate data before the move
03

Odoo Setup & Configuration

  • Install and configure your Odoo environment
  • Customize accounting, reporting, and other modules to your needs
  • Set up chart of accounts, tax rules, and fiscal settings
04

Data Migration

  • Securely migrate all data into Odoo
  • Validate data integrity record by record
  • Perform opening balance reconciliation
05

Testing & Training

  • End-to-end testing of all migrated data and workflows
  • Hands-on training for your accounting team
  • Written SOPs so nothing depends on memory
06

Go-Live & Ongoing Support

  • Managed transition to Odoo as your live system
  • Post-migration support and issue resolution
  • Optional: continued monthly bookkeeping inside Odoo
Why Make the Switch

What You Gain by Moving to Odoo

Upgrade from rigid legacy accounting to an integrated operational foundation.

Enhanced Efficiency

Automate the workflows that eat your time — invoicing, reconciliation, expense approvals — so your team focuses on higher-value work.

Add users, locations, entities, or entire business modules without changing systems. Odoo grows with you — QuickBooks doesn't.
