About Odoo

A Complete Business
Operating System

Odoo is a comprehensive open-source business management suite designed to cover every aspect of your company's operations. Originally launched in 2005, it now powers over 7 million users worldwide with hundreds of fully integrated applications.

Explore What is Odoo → View Core Modules
Platform Overview

What is Odoo?

An enterprise-grade open-source ERP framework built to unify every department into one streamlined, intelligent system.

Modular

Install only the apps you need, keeping the system lightweight and easy to scale as your business grows.

Web-Based

Run your entire business from a browser — no desktop installation, no IT overhead, accessible anywhere.

Open Source

No vendor lock-in. Full flexibility to adapt, extend, and own your platform as your needs evolve.

Customizable

Modify workflows, reports, and user roles to match your exact business processes and industry requirements.

Rapid to Deploy

Get up and running in weeks, not months — with expert guidance from the Pivot Aide implementation team.

Scalable

Start with what you need today. Add modules and users as your business expands — Odoo scales with you.

System Components

Key Odoo Modules

Odoo replaces multiple disconnected systems with a single, unified platform. Core modules most relevant to growing businesses:

CRM

Track leads, manage customers, and close deals faster with an integrated sales pipeline.

Sales & Invoicing

Create quotes, process orders, and generate invoices — all from one place.

Inventory Management

Optimize stock levels, automate logistics, and track procurement in real time.

Accounting

Real-time reporting, bank reconciliation, tax management, and automated invoicing.

HR & Payroll

Manage recruitment, onboarding, attendance, employee records, and payroll in one system.

eCommerce & Website

Launch and manage your online store with a built-in website builder and payment integrations.
